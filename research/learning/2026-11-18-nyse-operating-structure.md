**Proposed operating structure for Jason’s NYSE research bot — November 18, 2026**

The useful first product is one reproducible NYSE research run that produces an explained portfolio ledger and a clear decision about the evidence. The existing work supplies several components. The next implementation should connect them through one offline entry point, a common cash ledger and a report that Jason can review. This document proposes the structure and implementation order. Implementation and investment evaluation are pending.

The research mandate supplies $5,000 initial cash, whole common shares, a $1,000 per-position notional cap and 43 bps of stress on each traded side. Preserve applicable existing risk controls in the experiment specification. The rule being investigated is monthly momentum, comparing a top-five portfolio with a top-five portfolio that retains holdings through rank ten. Both arms need identical signals, eligibility, timing, accounting and risk policies so the retention rule is the intervention being measured.

PR #290’s runner, indicator code and tests remain on its draft branch. PR #292 contains the research record and data diagnostics. The implementation needs an isolated research checkout that reconciles the reviewed components from both branches before claiming the combined command works. Each published result records that checkout’s exact commit. [NYSE framework](https://github.com/Json2003/trading-bot/pull/290) · [Learning thread](https://github.com/Json2003/trading-bot/pull/292)

The first implementation should extend the NYSE runner to read a saved manifest independently of data fetching. Reuse the existing source hashing, report generation and tests where their behavior fits. The generic data store’s append-only symbol CSV convention needs versioned snapshots for this experiment because a corrected download must preserve the earlier research input. Keep large licensed inputs outside Git and retain their manifests and evidence links with the result. A single run should either produce the complete report or identify the exact missing field and affected calculation.

**Fix accounting before relying on portfolio returns.** Direct inspection of PR #290 shows that closing a position immediately adds proceeds to its cash balance. The runner also accumulates dividend receivables without payment dates and rejects split-bearing histories. Extend this ledger with settled cash, unsettled sale proceeds, dividend receivables and security-specific action events. A simulated purchase must satisfy the mandate’s settled-cash limit with fees reserved before whole-share sizing. A $1,000 cap is a ceiling on position notional, not a guarantee that every portfolio slot can be filled. [Inspected runner](https://github.com/Json2003/trading-bot/blob/236cf1cc41b01fc2edcdec901244c766ebbf4aa9/scripts/run_nyse_research_cycle.py)

Settlement uses the rule and business-day calendar applicable to each historical trade. The standard US cycle was T+2 in the January 2022 diagnostic and changed to T+1 on May 28, 2024. A dividend entitlement affects equity on its ex-date and becomes spendable under its payable/receipt treatment. Cash due for a merger or a fractional share also needs its own receipt assumption. A legal entitlement alone cannot establish the receipt date. [SEC settlement explanation](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/new-t1-settlement-cycle-what-investors-need-know-investor-bulletin)

The known DDS dividend, SLG split, CIT stock conversion, CSPR cash merger and CRY-to-AORT ticker change provide concrete regression cases for the ledger and data adapter. Acceptance includes preserved quantities and cost basis through a symbol change, exact share ratios with cash in lieu of fractions, no fills during suspension, no duplicate dividend income, and no premature reuse of proceeds. Synthetic rank/price fixtures can test selection and cash logic while historical inputs are incomplete. Their results establish software behavior. [Measured January evidence](../strategy_sources/2026-11-17-january-2022-coverage.md)

**Make the data requirement finite.** The 3,170-symbol audit is a coverage diagnostic. Resolving every listing in that archive is not a prerequisite for implementing or testing the software. A smaller universe can support a scoped diagnostic if its selection, dates and limitations are fixed before evaluating returns. It needs enough eligible candidates to exercise the top-ten boundary. Results then apply to that declared universe. Selecting today’s survivors, or dropping stocks whose future prices are missing, would bias the result. A broad NYSE claim needs historical membership and delisted securities.

Historical price diagnosis can proceed with documented execution assumptions. A result claiming executable returns needs defensible fills and size at the intended order times. First freeze the eligible ranks, intended orders and latency, then request the quotes needed for those orders. Preserve missing quotes, rejected fills and the unresolved candidates that could change the selected portfolio. This avoids downloading market-wide quotes as a prerequisite for every coding task. Any provider purchase, account or credential access remains an owner decision. The current public inputs have demonstrated coverage and corporate-action deficiencies.

Preserve raw executable prices separately from the price series used for momentum. Freeze the corporate-action convention and use information admissible at each decision. The proposed score close[t-21] / close[t-252] - 1 needs an observation at the 252-session lag, which means 253 indexed sessions through t. A provider’s adjusted OHLC series cannot automatically become the whole-share fill price. For example, Massive documents split-adjusted aggregate bars by default, raw bars with adjusted=false and no dividend adjustment. These are fields to verify in an adapter, not an endorsement of a particular subscription. [Official adjustment documentation](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/is-massives-stock-data-adjusted-for-splits-or-dividends)

**Give the daily learning task a measurable output.** The existing NYSE Edge Research automation was verified enabled for its daily schedule around 18:00 Pacific. Each cycle should advance the highest-priority unfinished item for the current comparison and record what was actually tested, the input/version cutoff, the evidence change and the next executable action. New source reading is appropriate when it resolves a dependency or tests a new credible hypothesis. A cycle without eligible new market observations should record that evaluation was skipped and complete an applicable code or source audit. Monthly decisions accumulate independent evidence on their own calendar. Daily reruns do not create additional monthly observations.

The owner-facing summary should answer six questions in one view.

1. Are the data usable through the required session, and what is missing?
2. Which frozen rule and universe are being evaluated?
3. What hypothetical positions, settled cash, pending proceeds and costs does the ledger show?
4. How do the two arms compare on net return, drawdown, Sharpe, profit factor, turnover and independent event counts?
5. What was learned today, and did the candidate gain or lose evidence?
6. What is the next action, who owns it, and does it require spending or trading authority?

Unavailable metrics should display as unmeasured with a reason. PR #290 reports max_drawdown, while the governed result wrapper expects drawdown. The result adapter therefore needs an explicit mapping and unit checks before it can advance the evaluation checkpoint. Field presence in the existing wrapper does not establish sample adequacy or profitability. Keep data-audit progress in the learning record and advance market-evaluation progress only after the relevant evaluation completes. Reruns should identify the experiment hash, data version and observation IDs so they cannot be counted twice. Preserve the existing applicable independent-observation minimum and validation gates. The legacy portfolio-target document contains other asset classes and does not authorize overriding NYSE risk controls.

A neural model becomes an additional candidate only after the baseline pipeline works. The readiness review found a missing training feature store, a scaler fitted before the split and unverified neural-to-order integration. A model comparison needs the same inputs, costs and ledger, a scaler fitted only on training data, chronological validation, saved weights and a documented training cutoff. For foundation models, the test interval must respect pretraining exposure. Fresh forward evidence begins after the actual experiment freeze and information cutoff. The old November 16 marker alone cannot establish an untouched test today. [Readiness review](https://github.com/Json2003/trading-bot/blob/236cf1cc41b01fc2edcdec901244c766ebbf4aa9/docs/equity_bot_readiness.md)

The immediate work order is to build and test the manifest-fed offline NYSE replay and settled-cash ledger, then implement the two-arm ranking comparison against that ledger. Complete the admissible momentum history and required order-time data before measuring a strategy result. The milestone is one repeatable research command whose saved report lets Jason follow each position and dollar and judge the evidence. A negative result still completes a valid experiment. Subsequent broker paper validation and capital allocation require their own measured readiness review and owner authorization.

This structure proposal adds no investment trials. It records implementation priorities and acceptance criteria for the existing research mandate. The research pipeline, combined entry point and new cash-ledger behavior are still to be implemented.
