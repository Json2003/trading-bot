# Trading education catalog

The current catalog contains **33 books and 41 total sources**. It is an expanding NYSE-stock research curriculum, not a completed reading record. Publisher/author overviews and contents were used to create bounded original lesson notes; complete commercial book texts have not been ingested, and neural weights have not been trained from them.

The stock research cycle retrieves relevant notes by skill tag and attaches their sources to its indicator report. No book, expert opinion or title count becomes a numeric trading signal. A new trading hypothesis still requires measured inputs, frozen rules, costs and genuinely unseen confirmation.

Study progression: verify source and access scope → extract a bounded lesson → map it to a capability and required data → implement and test the capability → freeze a hypothesis → collect future evidence. A catalog entry never implies that the bot has mastered or profited from the material.
