# Can the January 2022 data test the NYSE momentum hold buffer?

**Decision.** Use the historical listings and daily bars to investigate coverage. Reject this combination as the sole input to an executable backtest. A historical database revision omits a verified $15 cash dividend, two suspended stocks still appear in the listing snapshot at the open on their suspension date, and none of the freely inspected quote sources supplied the opening bid, ask and size needed to price an order. These errors affect which stock the strategy would rank, which order could fill and when the proceeds could be reused.

## What the data cover

We followed the January 3–31, 2022 window selected in the [preceding audit](2026-11-17-sec-security-class-audit.md). The starting denominator was 3,170 exact symbol strings in the January 1 NYSE snapshot, including non-common instruments and all 462 unresolved filer matches. Twenty regular sessions produced 63,400 possible symbol-days. Using a fixed denominator prevents a merger or a missing future price from silently improving apparent coverage. The denominator measures source coverage and is larger than the eventual eligible common-stock universe. For listings, we used the latest original Git revision with a committer timestamp at or before 09:30 New York time. This is a proxy for publication. Twenty distinct revisions covered the twenty opens. The oldest was 86.321 hours old. Of 49 starting symbols absent at least once, 30 were present again by the January 31 open. A rule treating first absence as a permanent exit would have misclassified those 30. The first February snapshot was isolated from the January counts. [Original listing history](https://github.com/rreichel3/US-Stock-Symbols/blob/b02d9d7ace317f861f8a6f59e2cfa88e207f3ce9/nyse/nyse_tickers.json)

For prices, we queried the first named Dolt OHLCV update for each session. All twenty revisions are timestamped on the next calendar date. Of 188,077 rows across all symbols, 53,519 matched the starting strings. A total of **53,376 (84.2% of possible cells)** had positive finite prices, open and close within the low–high range and positive integer volume. Of the 143 returned rows failing this screen, 142 failed the volume check and one additional row failed the price-range check. There were 456 starting strings with no daily bar on any session. Of the 462 previously unmatched strings, 454 had no exact bar match. These counts do not identify 456 delisted or eligible common stocks. Adjustment basis, opening venue and historical receipt time for the reported opens remain unverified. The February 1 split-update revision contained **zero January ex-date dividend rows** across the price database. These deliberately chosen event checks do not estimate the fraction of all corporate actions that are wrong.

## Can an opening fill be verified?

We parsed Nasdaq BX’s corrected January Rule 605 file. It contains 135,018 monthly rows and matches 2,715 starting ticker strings. The layout groups order types and sizes, starting at 100 shares. It has no session-level opening quote. A $1,000 position in a stock above $10 is already below that minimum order size. [Nasdaq BX report](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/content/MarketRegulation/BX/eqreport/bxeqreport_202201recalc.zip) · [Field definitions](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/content/MarketRegulation/SECRule605/appendixa1.pdf)

The pinned Hugging Face minute-bar directory listed 48 dates, all in 2016. Three January 2022 directory requests returned 404. A successful control download for CAT in 2016 yielded 408 OHLCV rows and no bid/ask fields. The card labels the dataset MIT. Upstream redistribution rights were unverified. The NYSE public Daily TAQ sample index had no January 2022 filename. Massive lists historical quote files for all twenty sessions behind an entitled account. File listings establish a possible source, while record contents and order-specific coverage still require verification. ## What this says about an edge

The top-five momentum and retain-through-rank-ten comparison is still untested. Under 43 bps per traded side, avoiding one $1,000 sale and one $1,000 replacement purchase would save $8.60 in modeled friction at those notionals. A retained stock can underperform its replacement by more than that. This is a cost illustration. Measured savings would require a paired order ledger. One month cannot supply the required independent monthly observations. Market-return evaluation was skipped. Investment trials, training runs and unseen confirmations were zero. Return, drawdown, Sharpe, profit factor, trades, turnover and realized costs were unmeasured.

**Next executable action.** Use the January 31 development decision to reconstruct the required 252-session input history. First reconcile security classes, dated corporate actions and quantities, using the five cases above as acceptance checks. Retain every missing or ambiguous candidate and show whether any could displace the top ten. Once eligibility, ranks and order times are defensible, freeze both arms’ intended orders before looking up the applicable next-session quotes. This limits quote retrieval to orders the strategies could have submitted and preserves rejected or unfilled orders. Keep the $5,000 settled-cash account, whole shares, $1,000 cap and existing validation thresholds throughout.
