# What the frozen retention test measures

The frozen hypothesis is that keeping eligible incumbents reduces trading costs enough to preserve or improve net results relative to monthly top-five selection. Novy-Marx and Velikov's December 2014 working-paper revision is a primary precedent. Their historical study used value-weighted long/short deciles and NYSE breakpoints on CRSP/Compustat common stocks across NYSE, AMEX and Nasdaq. Their appendix describes delisting adjustments and lags for accounting data. The full anomaly period was July 1963 to December 2012. The paper models effective spreads from daily prices, without a separate estimate of large-trade price impact. Its portfolio returns do not establish order-time quotes, next-open fill sizes, paid fees, or a whole-share, settled cash ledger. No associated replication data or code license was verified. [Working paper 20721, DOI 10.3386/w20721](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w20721/w20721.pdf)

The authors report that the 10% entry and 20% hold rule lowered momentum turnover from 34.52% to 18.82% monthly and modeled transaction costs from 65 to 35 basis points of portfolio return monthly. Average gross monthly excess return fell from 1.33% to 1.20%, while net rose from 0.68% to 0.85%. Net four-factor alpha fell from 0.40% (t = 3.12) to 0.13% (t = 1.52). The turnover and raw net-spread improvements do not establish incremental risk-adjusted returns. The authors' cost figures represent portfolio drag, with units distinct from charging 43 bps of each actual traded side. These are reported results, with zero independent reproductions. [Tables 3 and 8](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w20721/w20721.pdf)

The frozen comparison admits at most ten NYSE names and keeps incumbent ranks up to ten. Thus any incumbent that still passes eligibility and liquidity can remain a target after ranking. The other frozen exits and cash rules still apply. The paper restricts new entry to its entry band, while the project fills vacancies using the best remaining eligible names. The local comparison can measure the effect of retaining eligible holdings versus refreshing the top five. A positive result would have limited scope for judging tighter rank buffers, wider universes or transfer to executable after-cost returns.
