# Attributing results to trading costs in the frozen NYSE comparison

The testable hypothesis is that retaining eligible incumbents saves enough stress cost to offset any loss in gross marked P&L versus monthly top-five replacement. For a completed replay, measure gross marked P&L difference on the two actual sets of simulated fills, then add baseline stress expenses minus buffer stress expenses. That sum equals buffer minus baseline net marked P&L. Use the same $5,000 starting cash and divide by it for a portfolio return difference. The input specification and monthly decisions are frozen. ## Primary evidence and limits

Frazzini, Israel and Moskowitz's December 5, 2012 working-paper revision used one institutional manager's intended and executed trades from 1998–2011 across 19 developed markets to distinguish an execution-price effect from the cost of missed or delayed trades. Their portfolio optimization in section IV is a separate historical simulation across 1980–2011, using a liquid, cross-market universe and $100 million or $200 million starting fund sizes. Costs fitted on 1998–2011 data and applied to earlier years are retrospective estimates. The paper reports institutional net results. No public license to reuse the order-level records or verified replication inputs was found. The manager's orders supply neither a point-in-time listing and delisting dataset for this bot nor an estimate of retail next-open fills or charges. The local change uses the accounting distinction and leaves missed-order opportunity costs unestimated. [Working paper, sections I–IV and Tables V and VIII](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~afrazzin/pdf/Trading%20Cost%20of%20Asset%20Pricing%20Anomalies%20-%20Frazzini%2C%20Israel%20and%20Moskowitz.pdf)

## Local verification

The replay records realized net P&L on closed economic outcomes and the remainder in the unrealized balance. Both reconcile to ending equity less initial cash. The exact entry and exit stress fees remain a separate aggregate. Gross marked adds these charges back without changing fill times, quantities, settled cash rules or stress-cost sizing decisions. An isolated flat-price fixture verified that gross P&L was exactly zero and net marked P&L was exactly the negative of modeled expenses in each arm. All 34 focused NYSE software tests passed. The fixture used simulated prices and produced zero market trials or independent profit observations.
