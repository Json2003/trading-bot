## Measured source evidence

Channel 1 mapped 198 NYSE common stocks. Its 194 opening transactions all timestamped after 09:30 Eastern, from 09:30:00.732534031 to 09:38:26.673537493. Every transaction had an official-price companion with the same time, price and reported share count. All 197 official-price rows carried trade ID zero. Three official-price rows lacked an identical opening transaction row. For example, ELME's 558-share transaction at $1.49 and companion official price share a timestamp. Counting both as transactions would double the observed volume of that matched opening trade. Channel 10 contained 241,839 trade messages and zero NYSE-listed common-stock mappings, so channel identity must be verified before making any coverage claim.
