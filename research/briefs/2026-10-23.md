## 1. LOB-Impact Benchmark: Emergent Market Impact in Generative Limit Order Book Models

Published: 2026-10-19T18:01:00+00:00
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27100
Version: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.27100v1
Testing decision: No new market feature.

Across five generators and five NASDAQ stocks, injected metaorders produced
impact exponents of 0.63–1.21, but the tested models did not reproduce
post-execution impact decay. Visible-versus-blind injections distinguish learned
reactions from mechanical book depletion.

Hypothesis-design implication: matching historical spread/depth distributions
does not validate a simulator's response to the bot's own orders. Before using
a generated book for strategy evidence, require fixed injection, blind-control,
and relaxation checks. These are prospective evaluation recommendations; no
benchmark was installed or run here.

Limitations: equity evidence, no crypto replication, no market indicator feed.
No candidate JSON is appropriate.

## 2. Regime-Dependent Predictability of Cryptocurrency Distress: Nonlinear Evidence from Cross-Exchange Dynamics

Published: 2026-10-20 (publication date only; intraday release time unverified)
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/1911-8074/19/10/831
DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/jrfm19100831
Testing decision: No, pending a causal replication audit.

The paper reports regime-dependent relationships between cross-exchange spreads
and distress across four assets and three exchanges. Its retrospective regime
analysis uses smoothed probabilities conditioned on the full series; its
baseline distress threshold is also full-sample. It reports an expanding-window
threshold robustness check and a filtered MS-VAR forecasting benchmark.

Hypothesis-design implication: retrospective state separation is not itself an
available-at-decision indicator. Those robustness checks do not establish that
every stage of the full pipeline is causal. Require a reproducible audit of
regime estimation, target thresholds, transformations and forecast timestamps
before nomination. This concern does not establish that all reported forecasts
are invalid.

Data gap: complete point-in-time replication remains unverified; direct full-page
retrieval was rate limited, so assessment uses indexed publisher methods text.
No candidate JSON or numeric feed is nominated.
