## 1. Stochastic Modelling of High-frequency (HF) Crypto Limit Order Book (LOB) with General Compound Marked Hawkes Process

- Authors: Mine Çağlar, Serhat Güldoğan, Anatoliy Swishchuk.
- Published: November 16, 2026, 10:19:43 UTC.
- Primary sources: [arXiv v1 record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20839v1), [full text, sections 4–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.20839v1).
- Finding: The model couples event clustering with variable price increments, fitted to about 12 days of BTC Level-1 observations from April 2021. Estimated self-excitation is 0.75, not a causal liquidation statistic.
- Hypothesis implication: Compare arrival-intensity features with a recent-event-count baseline.
- Testing: **No** new candidate. In-sample diffusion fit is not prospective prediction or profit; independent confirmation, stress-cost evidence and a receipt-time feed are absent.

## 2. When is the order book thinnest? Weekends and the daily break

- Source: [Swiftedge original study and methodology](https://swiftedge.uk/research/order-book-liquidity-by-hour).
- Published: November 5, 2026; materially updated November 16, 2026. Exact release time/offset is not supplied.
- Finding: Across three weekends, reported crypto weekend depth was 96–101% of neighboring-weekday depth. BTC's hourly profile was unstable between recordings.
- Hypothesis implication: Do not assume a fixed weekend/hour liquidity penalty; condition any later hypothesis on measured, venue-specific depth.
- Testing: **No** new candidate. Only 16–17 daily observations per hour; venue mixture and availability timing are insufficiently specified. Displayed depth is not executable liquidity. Clock/session descriptions need reconciliation. The linked aggregate CSV does not repair these gaps.
