## 1. An Empirical Study of Undisclosed Charges in Hook-Based Decentralized Exchanges

Published: 2026-10-27T05:52:06+00:00
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36682
Testing decision: No new market-signal candidate.

The authors reconstruct Hook-related settlement deductions across Uniswap v4
and PancakeSwap Infinity, using 7,873 deployments, 50,011 pools and 296 million
pool events through August 1, 2026. They report charging capability in 24.30%
of Hooks and deductions in 59.23% of accounted swaps involving those Hooks.
These are conditional denominators, not a percentage of all DEX swaps.

Hypothesis implication: AMM execution research must account for actual settlement
deductions in addition to displayed pool fees. The study does not establish
return prediction or transfer to centralized perpetuals.

Data gap: historical deductions become known at settlement. Using them as a
pre-trade feature without a verified quote-time simulation would leak future
information. No production trades or simulations were run here.

## 2. Cryptocurrency Trading: Why Low Fees Don't Always Mean Low Costs

Research summary published: 2026-10-27 (time of day unverified)
Source: https://www.frankfurt-school.de/en/knowledge/research/cryptocurrency-trading-why-low-fees-dont-always-mean-low-costs
Underlying study: The Retail Costs of Cryptocurrency Trading, Olaf Stotz.
Testing decision: No new market-signal candidate.

The university reports 2,088 actual buy/sell transactions across six German
brokers over 52 weeks. Average immediate round-trip costs ranged from 1.23%
to 4.58%, with broker fees and markups explaining much of the difference.

Hypothesis implication: a nominal stress-cost assumption can understate actual
venue costs. Preserve the shared 86-bps test, but separately reject simulated
profit that cannot cover measured execution charges. The reported figures
do not estimate the bot's IBKR or crypto-exchange costs.

Limitations: university summary rather than independently reproduced full
transaction data; retail brokers and immediate round trips differ from the
bot's instruments, venues and holding periods. No cost coefficient was changed.
