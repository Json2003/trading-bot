- Published: November 10, 2026, 14:07:39 UTC (`2026-11-10T14:07:39+00:00`).
- Finding: A model pretrained on Bitstamp/Bitfinex Level-3 events improves sub-second microprice forecasting. Predicted moves remain below one basis point and fail the paper's approximately 2-bps one-way cost comparison. Directional evaluation covers 37 hours of disjoint holdouts within four weeks on one instrument/venue.
- Hypothesis implication: Event sequences may add information beyond a book snapshot, but forecast correlation is not executable expectancy. Keep event-clock reconstruction and availability audits separate from any profitability claim.

## 2. Beyond Anonymous Order Flow: Wallet Identity as a Feature for Short-Horizon Return Prediction

- Source: Jingqiu Ding and Daojing Zhai, [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05218v1), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.05218v1).
- Published: November 3, 2026, 17:03:36 UTC (`2026-11-03T17:03:36+00:00`). This is a recent source since the last verified pushed brief, October 30; it is not a release from the past seven days.
- Finding: Hyperliquid wallet-attributed events raise one-second ridge out-of-sample R² from 10.88% to 12.31% relative to an anonymous benchmark. The July evaluation spans seven days; cohort selection, scaling and window choices were retrospective.
- Hypothesis implication: Previously measured wallet behavior could distinguish otherwise similar aggregate flow. A clean experiment would need receipt-time availability and independently frozen cohort membership before future observations.
- Testing: **No** for a bot candidate now. Consensus timestamps do not establish bot availability; stale states cross data gaps, and a fully gap-masked rerun remains outstanding. Statistical prediction is not fill-adjusted profit. These results supply neither untouched forward confirmation nor positive expectancy after the bot's costs.
